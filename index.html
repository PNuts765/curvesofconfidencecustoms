How The Experience Works

1. Send Your Photos

Send clear high quality photos with different angles of your face and body. Blurry, dark, cropped, or low quality images may not be approved. You can also send already glamorous photos if you already feel ready to be published because you're hot ♡

2. Outfit & Pose Direction

You may send outfit inspiration, pose ideas, themes, aesthetic moods, hairstyles, luxury concepts, or specific visions you want included. If you don’t know what poses or outfits to choose, creative direction can be built for you.

3. Approval Process

After your materials are reviewed and approved, you’ll receive the official PayPal payment link directly by email. Payment is only accepted AFTER proper materials are approved.

4. Luxury Creation

Your custom experience is then fully designed into a cohesive luxury book with elegant artistic styling, femininity, confidence, beautiful compositions, and collectible presentation.

5. Time Frame

Luxury custom work takes time. Estimated completion time is approximately 3–4 weeks depending on detail, communication, revisions, and project complexity.
